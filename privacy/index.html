Privacy Policy

פְּרָטִיּוּת

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version: Torat Mayim doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

Torat Mayim is an app that teaches the words of Torah, Navi, Mishna, Talmud, Rashi and Tosafot, built by David Lebee. This policy covers the Torat Mayim apps and this website.

What the app stores

Torat Mayim saves which words you've studied, how your quiz answers went, and your settings (language, nikud, Rashi script, voice speed and reminder times). All of it stays on your phone. We don't have a server, so we never see it. Deleting the app deletes it. If your phone backs up apps to iCloud, this data may be included in that backup, which Apple's privacy terms cover.

What the app doesn't collect

We don't collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, usage analytics or crash reports. The app has no sign-in, no in-app purchases, no advertising and no third-party code that tracks anyone. It doesn't use the microphone or the camera.

Audio

Words and lines are read aloud by your phone's built-in Hebrew text-to-speech, which runs on the phone.

Reminders

If you turn on study reminders, Torat Mayim schedules them as notifications on your phone, at the hour and on the days you choose. They're scheduled on the phone itself; nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Children

Torat Mayim collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Beta testing

If you test a pre-release version through Apple TestFlight, Apple may share basic information with us, such as your name or email, the device you used, crash reports, and any feedback you choose to send. We use it only to fix and improve the app. Apple's privacy policy covers how Apple handles that data.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, GitHub and Google may log your IP address and browser details when you visit. We don't add any analytics or cookies of our own.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub, or see the support page.
