Support

עֶזְרָה

Need help, or spotted a wrong meaning? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include the text (Torah, Talmud…), the word, and what you'd expect. Corrections to meanings and translations are especially welcome.

Common questions

The Hebrew isn't read aloud.

Torat Mayim uses your iPhone's Hebrew voice. Go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Hebrew and download a voice (Carmit is a good one), then try again.

How do I turn the vowels (nikud) off?

In Torat Mayim, open Settings and turn off Show nikud (vowels). Turn it back on any time.

Can I see Rashi and Tosafot in square letters?

Yes. In Settings, turn off Rashi script for Rashi and Tosafot.

How do I change the language?

Torat Mayim follows your phone's language (English or French). To choose a different one, open Settings in Torat Mayim and pick a language.

My reminders don't arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for Torat Mayim in the iPhone Settings app, and that today is one of the days you chose. By default reminders skip Friday and Shabbat.

What does "in review" mean next to a meaning?

Meanings and translations are written for Torat Mayim and checked by reviewers. Ones not yet checked are marked "in review". If one looks wrong, please tell us.

Does Torat Mayim sync between devices?

No. Your progress stays on the phone, with no account. It's included in your iCloud backup if you use one.
