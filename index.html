Why the name

Two ways water teaches.

The Gemara says "water" means Torah. Two stories show how it flows.

Rabbi Akiva · the drop

Water wears through stone, one drop at a time.

מָה רַךְ פּוֹסֵל אֶת הַקָּשֶׁה, דִּבְרֵי תוֹרָה שֶׁקָּשִׁין כַּבַּרְזֶל עַל אַחַת כַּמָּה וְכַמָּה

Rabbi Akiva was forty and had never learned when he saw a stone hollowed by dripping water. If soft water can cut stone, he reasoned, words of Torah can reach my heart (Avot de-Rabbi Natan 6). So it goes here: a few words each day.

Be'er Avraham · the well

Dig again the wells of your fathers.

וַיָּשָׁב יִצְחָק וַיַּחְפֹּר אֶת בְּאֵרֹת הַמַּיִם אֲשֶׁר חָפְרוּ בִּימֵי אַבְרָהָם אָבִיו

Isaac dug again the wells Avraham had dug, and called them by the same names (Genesis 26:18). Each text here is such a well: the generations dug it, and you open it again, word by word, as the water rises.

How it works

Study a little. Quiz a little. Read more every day.

  1. בְּחַר

    Pick a text

    Torah, Navi, Mishna, Talmud, Rashi or Tosafot. Each has its own list, ranked by how often its words appear.

  2. לְמַד

    Study

    Each word comes with its meaning, its common forms, and a real line from the text where it appears, with a translation.

  3. חֲזוֹר

    Quiz

    Quiz single words, then whole phrases. Words you've learned keep coming back, so they stay learned.

  4. קְרָא

    Read

    Watch the count grow: how many verses you can now follow, or how many words on a page of Gemara you know.

Questions

Questions, answered.

Who is Torat Mayim for?

Anyone who can sound out Hebrew letters and wants to understand what they're reading: in shul, preparing a parsha, starting Mishna, or opening a Gemara for the first time. If you already learn, it's a quick way to fill in the words you skip over.

Do I need to know Hebrew?

You need to be able to read the letters. Torat Mayim teaches the meanings. Nikud is on by default, and every example line comes with a translation.

Why "most frequent first"?

A small set of words makes up most of any text. Learning them first means every hour of study shows up the next time you open the text. In the Torah, Torat Mayim's first 978 words cover 92% of what you'll read.

Why are Torah words grouped by root?

Hebrew builds words from three-letter roots. Learning אָמַר together with its family of forms sticks better than meeting each form on its own.

Are the meanings reliable?

They're written for learners, using Strong's, Brown-Driver-Briggs and Jastrow. Meanings and translations still being checked are marked "in review" in the app. Spot a mistake? Tell us on the support page.

How much does it cost?

Torat Mayim is free, with no ads, no account and no in-app purchases.

Which devices does it run on?

Torat Mayim is coming to iPhone first. Android is planned.
